    \textbf{Key Learning Goals:}
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item \textbf{AI Concepts:} Understand how AI learns through Supervised Learning, Large Language Models, and Computer Vision.
        \item \textbf{Real-World Application:} Match different AI types to real problems in sustainability and ecology.
        \item \textbf{Critical Thinking:} Differentiate between human and AI-generated content.
        \item \textbf{Design Thinking:} Prototype a conceptual AI solution for a community problem.
    \end{enumerate}
\end{keyinfobox}

\section*{Learning Outcomes}
Students will be able to:

\subsubsection*{KNOWLEDGE \& UNDERSTANDING:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Define Artificial Intelligence and describe in simple terms how it learns.
    \item Identify key AI learning paradigms such as supervised learning, Computer Vision, and Large Language Models.
    \item Understand that AI can be used to support innovation in fields like sustainability.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{SKILLS \& ABILITIES:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Differentiate between human and AI-generated content.
    \item Work collaboratively to research and present ideas.
    \item Design a basic concept for an AI-powered solution.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{ATTITUDES \& VALUES:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Develop critical thinking about AI capabilities and limitations.
    \item Understand the importance of safety, ethics, and responsibility when using AI.
\end{itemize}

\section{Introduction and Motivation}
\textbf{Goal:} Build a shared understanding of what AI is.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Start with a short video and discussion about where students encounter AI in everyday life.
    \item Ask what AI can do well and what concerns students may already have.
    \item Use a quick quiz to assess prior knowledge.
\end{itemize}

\section{Research and Learning}
\textbf{Goal:} Explore different ways AI learns and where it is used.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Introduce supervised learning, Large Language Models, and Computer Vision in simplified language.
    \item In pairs, students research a field such as healthcare, environment, or smart cities.
    \item Students identify which AI paradigm is being used and explain why.
    \item Play ``Find the AI Impostor'' where students compare human and AI-generated answers.
\end{itemize}

\section{Creative Application}
\textbf{Goal:} Apply AI ideas to real-world problems.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Begin with a matching game that pairs real-world problems with suitable AI solutions.
    \item In small groups, students choose a local or ecological problem and design a conceptual AI solution.
    \item Each group explains the problem, the proposed AI system, its learning method, and possible risks.
\end{itemize}

\section{Reflection and Evaluation}
\textbf{Goal:} Consolidate understanding and connect it to responsible AI use.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Students complete a self-assessment rubric.
    \item Reflection questions can include: ``What was the most surprising thing I learned about AI?'' and ``How can we use AI in a fair and safe way?''
    \item End with a short discussion of benefits, risks, and responsible choices.
\end{itemize}
