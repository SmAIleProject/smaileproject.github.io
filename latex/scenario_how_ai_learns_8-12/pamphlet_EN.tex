\pamphletheading{Introduction}
How does AI learn?

In this workshop, students (8-12) explore how AI ``thinks'' and learns, discover different AI types like Computer Vision and chatbots, and design their own ideas for solving real-world problems.

\vfill\null
\pamphletheading{Key Goals}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=SmaileLightBlue, colframe=SmaileBlue, boxrule=0.5pt, arc=2mm]
\small
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Understand:} How AI learns.
    \item \textbf{Identify:} Different AI types.
    \item \textbf{Compare:} Human and AI answers.
    \item \textbf{Design:} An AI solution idea.
\end{itemize}
\end{tcolorbox}

\columnbreak
\pamphletheading{Resources}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Video:} Introductory AI examples.
    \item \textbf{Game:} AI matching cards and impostor challenge.
    \item \textbf{Tech:} Laptops/tablets and projector.
    \item \textbf{Support:} Worksheet, checklist, timer, and reflection form.
\end{itemize}

\pamphletheading{1. AI in Our Lives}
\textbf{Video and Quiz:}
Students discuss where they meet AI, watch a short intro video, and test prior knowledge with a quick quiz.

\pamphletheading{2. The AI Impostor}
\textbf{Game and Research:}
Students compare human and AI-generated answers and research different AI paradigms and industries.

\pamphletheading{3. Design Challenge}
\textbf{Solve a Problem:}
Teams choose a real problem, match it to a type of AI, and present a safe, fair solution idea.
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=SmaileGray, boxrule=0.5pt]
\small
\textbf{Discussion:}
``How can we use AI in a fair and safe way?''
\end{tcolorbox}
