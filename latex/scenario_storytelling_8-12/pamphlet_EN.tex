\pamphletheading{Introduction}
How did the Age of Discovery change our world?

In this workshop, students (8-12) will become explorers, map-makers, and storytellers. They will use AI tools to bring history to life and discover the origins of the world we know today.

\vfill\null
\pamphletheading{Key Goals}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=SmaileLightBlue, colframe=SmaileBlue, boxrule=0.5pt, arc=2mm]
\small
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Analyze:} Historical events and figures.
    \item \textbf{Map:} Routes of famous explorers.
    \item \textbf{Create:} Diaries using AI visuals.
    \item \textbf{Reflect:} On cultural exchange and heritage.
\end{itemize}
\end{tcolorbox}

% --- Panel 5: Activities ---
\pamphletheading{1. Research}
\textbf{Explorer Profiles:}
Teams research Columbus, Da Gama, or Magellan using AI tools.
\textbf{Mapping Routes:}
Using Google My Maps to trace journeys.
\textbf{Food Origins:}
"Why is there cinnamon in my roll?" Investigating how trade changed European cuisine.

% --- Panel 6: Creative ---
\pamphletheading{2. Creative Application}
\textbf{The Diary of an Explorer:}
Students write journals describing their fears and discoveries.
\textbf{AI Visuals:}
Using AI to generate illustrations that match their written descriptions.
