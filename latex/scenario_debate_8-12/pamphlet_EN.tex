\pamphletheading{Introduction}
How do we make smart decisions about AI?

In this workshop, students (8-12) develop critical thinking skills by debating complex topics like AI surveillance and chatbots. They learn to research facts, listen to opposing views, and speak with confidence!

\vfill\null

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=SmaileLightPurple, colframe=SmailePurple, boxrule=0.5pt, arc=2mm]

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Research:} Finding evidence.
    \item \textbf{Argue:} Persuasive speaking.
    \item \textbf{Listen:} Respectful rebuttal.
    \item \textbf{Decide:} Ethical thinking.
\end{itemize}

% --- Panel 5: Activities ---
\pamphletheading{1. Preparation}
\textbf{Research:}
Teams use safe sources to find facts supporting their side (Affirmative or Negative).
\textbf{Topics:}
"Should schools use AI cameras?"
"Are Chatbots good for homework?"

\pamphletheading{2. The Debate}
\textbf{Rounds:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Constructive:} Making the case.
    \item \textbf{Crossfire:} Asking questions.
    \item \textbf{Rebuttal:} Defending arguments.
\end{itemize}

% --- Panel 6: Reflection ---
\pamphletheading{3. Evaluation}
\textbf{Judging:}
Using a rubric to score arguments and evidence quality.
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=SmaileOrange, boxrule=0.5pt]
\small
\textbf{Reflection:}
"What was the strongest argument you heard today?"
\end{tcolorbox}
