In this workshop, children (4-7) will team up with "Brainy" (an AI helper) to create amazing adventure stories. They will learn how to build a tale, work together, and use their imagination to explore new worlds!

% --- Panel 5: Activities ---
\pamphletheading{1. The Big Idea}
\textbf{What is a story?}
We learn about beginnings, middles, and ends. We meet "Brainy," our robot friend who loves stories but needs our ideas!

\pamphletheading{2. Our Adventure}
\textbf{Card Pick:}
Groups choose a Character (e.g., Unicorn) and a Place (e.g., Cave).
\textbf{AI Prompt:}
Brainy suggests a funny situation: \textit{"The unicorn lost its horn in the dark cave!"}
\textbf{Create:}
Children draw what happens next.

% --- Panel 6: Creative ---
\pamphletheading{3. Sharing Time}
\textbf{Tell the Tale:}
Groups stand up and act out their story using their drawings.
