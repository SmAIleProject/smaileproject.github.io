\pamphletheading{Introduction}
Inside the Black Box.

In this workshop, students (13-16) go beyond the hype to understand how AI actually works. Through hands-on simulations with Machine Learning and Neural Networks, they learn to differentiate between AI types and design their own conceptual solutions for real-world problems.

\vfill\null
\pamphletheading{Key Goals}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=SmaileLightBlue, colframe=SmaileBlue, boxrule=0.5pt, arc=2mm]
\small
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Train:} Build models with Teachable Machine.
    \item \textbf{Identify:} AI types (Vision, LLMs).
    \item \textbf{Solve:} Design AI for sustainability.
    \item \textbf{Reflect:} On ethics and bias.
\end{itemize}
\end{tcolorbox}

% --- Panel 5: Activities ---
\pamphletheading{1. The Black Box}
\textbf{Teachable Machine:}
Students train a computer to recognize images or sounds.
\textbf{Quick, Draw!:}
Interacting with a neural network to see how it "thinks."

\pamphletheading{2. Real World AI}
\textbf{Matching Game:}
Connecting technologies (like Computer Vision) to real problems (like detecting forest fires).

% --- Panel 6: Reflection ---
\pamphletheading{3. Design Challenge}
\textbf{Solve a Problem:}
Teams identify a sustainability issue and propose an AI-powered solution.

\small
\textbf{Discussion:}
