\section*{Learning Outcomes}
By the end of the project, students will be able to:

\subsubsection*{KNOWLEDGE \& UNDERSTANDING:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Define Artificial Intelligence and articulate how it learns from data.
    \item Identify key paradigms: Supervised Learning, LLMs, Computer Vision.
    \item Recognize various AI types and match them to appropriate problems.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{SKILLS \& ABILITIES:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Develop critical thinking to differentiate human vs. AI content.
    \item Use tools like Teachable Machine to train basic models.
    \item Design conceptual AI solutions for sustainability issues.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{ATTITUDES \& VALUES:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Understand AI as a tool for innovation, not just automation.
    \item Reflect on ethical considerations: privacy, bias, and accountability.
\end{itemize}

% --- Activities ---
\section{Introduction: What is AI?}
\textbf{Goal:} Establish a clear definition.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Discussion:} "What tasks are easy for you but hard for a robot?"
    \item \textbf{Concept:} AI is not magic; it is a system that learns patterns from data.
\end{itemize}

\section{Activity 1: The "Black Box" of Machine Learning}
\textbf{Goal:} Experiential learning.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Teachable Machine:} Students train a model to recognize specific gestures or sounds. They see how "more data" improves accuracy.
    \item \textbf{Quick, Draw!:} Students interact with a neural network that guesses their drawings.
    \item \textbf{Key Takeaway:} Machines learn from examples (training data).
\end{itemize}

\section{Activity 2: AI in the Real World}
\textbf{Goal:} Application of knowledge.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Matching Game:} Match the AI Technology (e.g., Computer Vision) to the Use Case (e.g., Sorting recycling).
    \item \textbf{Discussion:} How does Netflix know what you want to watch? (Recommender Systems).
\end{itemize}

\section{Activity 3: Solve a Problem with AI}
\textbf{Goal:} Innovation and design.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Challenge:} Choose a sustainability problem (e.g., plastic waste, energy usage).
    \item \textbf{Solution:} Design an AI concept to solve it.
    \item \textbf{Pitch:} "We will use [AI Type] to [Action] in order to [Goal]."
    \item \textbf{Ethics Check:} Identify potential risks (privacy, cost).
\end{itemize}

\section{Reflection and Evaluation}
\textbf{Goal:} Critical reflection.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Self-Assessment:} Complete the provided rubric.
    \item \textbf{Discussion:} "Do we trust AI to make important decisions?"
    \item \textbf{Future:} How will AI change the careers students are interested in?
\end{itemize}
