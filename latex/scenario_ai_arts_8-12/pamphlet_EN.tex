\pamphletheading{Introduction}
Can AI be creative?

In this workshop, students (8-12) explore the intersection of art and technology. They will study famous masters, create their own digital art, and collaborate with AI to generate images and music for a virtual exhibition.

\vfill\null
\pamphletheading{Key Goals}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=SmaileLightPink, colframe=SmailePink, boxrule=0.5pt, arc=2mm]
\small
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Create:} Digital art \& AI music.
    \item \textbf{Study:} Van Gogh, Miró, Kandinsky.
    \item \textbf{Compare:} Human vs. AI art.
    \item \textbf{Exhibit:} Virtual Gallery (Padlet).
\end{itemize}
\end{tcolorbox}

% --- Panel 5: Activities ---
\pamphletheading{1. Create \& Compare}
\textbf{Human Art:}
Students draw in the style of a master (e.g., Van Gogh's swirls).
\textbf{AI Art:}
Students describe the same style to an AI.
\textbf{Venn Diagram:}
Comparing the results. Which had more "soul"? Which was faster?

% --- Panel 6: Creative ---
\pamphletheading{2. Soundtrack}
\textbf{AI Music:}
Using tools like Suno to generate a soundtrack that fits the mood of their painting.

\pamphletheading{3. Exhibition}
\textbf{Virtual Gallery:}
A class event where works are displayed on Padlet.
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=SmaileOrange, boxrule=0.5pt]
\small
\textbf{Reflection:}
"What is the difference between a human artist and a robot?"
