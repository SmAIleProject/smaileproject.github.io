    \begin{enumerate}

        \item \textbf{Art History:} Learn about styles of famous artists (e.g., Van Gogh, Miró).

% --- Outcomes ---
\section*{Learning Outcomes}
By the end of the project, students will be able to:

\subsubsection*{KNOWLEDGE \& UNDERSTANDING:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Recognize styles and colors used by famous artists.
    \item Understand how AI tools can assist in creating images and music.
    \item Understand how music influences the perception of visual art.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{SKILLS \& ABILITIES:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Create digital artwork based on specific artistic styles.
    \item Use AI prompts to generate media and compare it with manual creations.
    \item Present work digitally using tools like Padlet or Canva.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{ATTITUDES \& VALUES:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Develop curiosity about the intersection of art and technology.
    \item Appreciate diverse forms of expression.
    \item Collaborate effectively in teams to curate an exhibition.
\end{itemize}

% --- Activities ---
\section{Week 1: Introduction to Art and AI}
\textbf{Goal:} Understand artistic styles.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Slideshow:} Present paintings by Van Gogh, Kandinsky, Miró.
    \item \textbf{Discussion:} "How do these colors make you feel?" "What shapes do you see?"
    \item \textbf{AI Intro:} Watch a video on AI image generation. Discuss: "Can a robot be an artist?"
\end{itemize}

\section{Week 2: Drawing and Generating}
\textbf{Goal:} Human vs. Machine creativity.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Human Creation:} Students use Paint/Canva to create a drawing inspired by a famous artist.
    \item \textbf{AI Creation:} Students write a text prompt describing their drawing to an AI tool.
    \item \textbf{Compare:} Use a Venn Diagram to spot similarities and differences between the two.
\end{itemize}

\section{Week 3: Music and Atmosphere}
\textbf{Goal:} Multi-sensory art.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Prompting Music:} Students use AI music generators (like Suno) to create a 30-second clip that matches the "mood" of their painting.
    \item \textbf{Selection:} Teams vote on the best track for their artwork.
\end{itemize}

\section{Week 4: Virtual Exhibition}
\textbf{Goal:} Celebration and Reflection.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Setup:} Upload Human Art, AI Art, and Music to Padlet.
    \item \textbf{Gallery Walk:} The class views the digital gallery.
    \item \textbf{Presentation:} Groups share their "Artist Statement."
    \item \textbf{Reflection:} Fill out assessment rubrics (included in attachments).
\end{itemize}
