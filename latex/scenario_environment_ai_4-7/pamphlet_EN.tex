\pamphletheading{Introduction}
Where does a robot's energy come from?

In this 3-week workshop, young children (4-7) discover where energy comes from, how tablets and robots use electricity, and how AI can help us build a greener planet.

\vfill\null
\pamphletheading{Key Goals}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=SmaileLightGreen, colframe=SmaileGreen, boxrule=0.5pt, arc=2mm]
\small
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Learn:} Where energy comes from.
    \item \textbf{Notice:} How technology uses electricity.
    \item \textbf{Imagine:} Robots helping nature.
    \item \textbf{Create:} An eco-city together.
\end{itemize}
\end{tcolorbox}

\columnbreak
\pamphletheading{Resources}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Stories:} Energy and environment picture books.
    \item \textbf{Game:} Energy Collector cards and stickers.
    \item \textbf{Build:} Lego, paper, crayons, or Minecraft.
    \item \textbf{Tech:} Tablets or laptops when available.
\end{itemize}

\pamphletheading{1. Energy Collector Game}
\textbf{Learning Through Play:}
Children pick illustrated task cards, collect energy stickers, and compare which activities use more electricity.

\pamphletheading{2. Helping the Planet}
\textbf{Eco-City Challenge:}
Groups draw or build a city with green spaces, solar panels, and robot helpers.

\pamphletheading{3. Reflection}
\textbf{Closing Circle:}
Children share what surprised them and one thing they can do at home to save energy.
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=SmaileGray, boxrule=0.5pt]
\small
\textbf{Discussion:}
``How can robots help take care of our planet?''
\end{tcolorbox}
