\section*{Learning Outcomes}
By the end of the workshop, children will be able to:

\subsubsection*{KNOWLEDGE \& UNDERSTANDING:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Understand that energy comes from resources like water, sun, and wind.
    \item Recognize that computers and robots need energy to work.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{SKILLS \& ABILITIES:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Work in pairs or teams to play games and count energy points.
    \item Share ideas through drawing, storytelling, or role-play.
    \item Build models with Lego, paper, or Minecraft to express eco-friendly ideas.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{ATTITUDES \& VALUES:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Show care for the environment through small daily actions.
    \item Appreciate how innovation can help build a greener future.
\end{itemize}

\section{Working Methods}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Storytelling:} Using books to introduce abstract concepts like energy.
    \item \textbf{Gamification:} Making energy use visible through an ``Energy Collector Game''.
    \item \textbf{Creative Play:} Building eco-cities to visualize solutions.
    \item \textbf{Reflection:} Sharing surprises and simple energy-saving pledges for home.
\end{itemize}

\section{Introduction and Motivation}
\textbf{Goal:} Introduce the idea that technology uses energy.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Welcome children to a lesson about robots, computers, and energy.
    \item Read a story such as ``Energy Makes Things Happen'' or ``Planet Power''.
    \item Discuss questions like: ``Does a tablet need food?'' and ``Where does electricity come from?''
    \item Invite children to draw or point to things that use energy at home.
\end{itemize}

\section{Research and Learning}
\textbf{Goal:} Understand that some actions use more energy than others.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Divide children into two or three teams.
    \item Place illustrated task cards face down, for example robot vacuum, playing music, or watching video.
    \item Teams pick cards and collect stickers that represent energy use.
    \item Count the stickers together and talk about which tasks use more energy and why.
\end{itemize}

\section{Creative Application}
\textbf{Goal:} Show that technology can help the planet.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Ask: ``How can robots help nature?''
    \item Children work in small groups to build or draw an eco-city.
    \item Encourage green spaces, recycling robots, and solar panels.
    \item Let each group present one idea, for example ``Our robot collects rubbish to help animals.'' 
\end{itemize}

\section{Reflection and Evaluation}
\textbf{Goal:} End with practical, child-friendly action.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Children share orally or through drawing what surprised them.
    \item Ask what they can do at home, such as turning off lights or unplugging a tablet.
    \item Encourage positive feedback for every eco-friendly idea.
\end{itemize}
