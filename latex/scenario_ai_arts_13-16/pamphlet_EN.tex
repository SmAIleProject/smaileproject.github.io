\pamphletheading{Introduction}
Curating Creativity.

In this workshop, students (13-16) explore the cutting edge of art and technology. They become digital curators, creating original art, collaborating with AI generators, and composing soundtracks to build an immersive virtual exhibition.

\vfill\null
\pamphletheading{Key Goals}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=SmaileLightPink, colframe=SmailePink, boxrule=0.5pt, arc=2mm]

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Create:} Digital Art \& Music.
    \item \textbf{Compare:} Human vs. AI.
    \item \textbf{Curate:} Virtual Gallery.
    \item \textbf{Reflect:} On creative ethics.
\end{itemize}
\end{tcolorbox}

% --- Panel 5: Activities ---
\pamphletheading{1. The Studio}
\textbf{Human vs Machine:}
Students create a piece manually, then use AI to generate a version based on their description.
\textit{Does the AI capture the same emotion?}

\pamphletheading{2. Sonic Landscapes}
\textbf{AI Music:}
Composing a background track that matches the mood (tempo, instruments) of the visual art.

% --- Panel 6: Reflection ---
\pamphletheading{3. Exhibition}
\textbf{Gallery Walk:}
Presenting the final multimedia project on Padlet for peer review.
