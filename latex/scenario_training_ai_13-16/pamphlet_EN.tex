\pamphletheading{Introduction}
Teaching Machines to See.

In this workshop, students (13-16) explore the intersection of AI and Science. By building their own datasets and training models to classify scientific objects (like rocks or plants), they gain a practical understanding of Machine Learning and the ethical challenges of automation.

% --- Panel 5: Activities ---
\pamphletheading{1. Data Collection}
\textbf{Fieldwork:}
Students collect images of specific scientific categories (e.g., igneous vs. sedimentary rocks), ensuring the data is diverse and high-quality.

\pamphletheading{2. Training \& Testing}
\textbf{Teachable Machine:}
Students feed their data into the tool to train a model. They then test it with new, unseen samples to verify its accuracy.

% --- Panel 6: Reflection ---
\pamphletheading{3. Ethics}
\textbf{Debate:}
"Can AI replace lab scientists?"
Students discuss the reliability of AI in critical tasks and the importance of human oversight.
