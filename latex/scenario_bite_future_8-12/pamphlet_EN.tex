\pamphletheading{Introduction}
What will we eat in 2050?

In this workshop, students (8-12) explore the future of food. They will research strange innovations like 3D-printed snacks and vertical farms, then use their creativity to design a delicious, sustainable menu for the future!

% --- Panel 5: Activities ---
\pamphletheading{1. Strange Food}
\textbf{Research Teams:}
Students investigate topics like Vertical Farming or Edible Insects. They find facts to share: "Did you know crickets have lots of protein?"

\pamphletheading{2. Future Menu}
\textbf{Design Challenge:}
Teams create a menu for a restaurant in 2050.
\textbf{Rules:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Must be healthy.
    \item Must be sustainable (Eco-friendly).
    \item Must look yummy!
\end{itemize}
