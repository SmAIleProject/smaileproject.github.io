\pamphletheading{Introduction}
Is the internet invisible?

In this workshop, students (8-12) discover the physical side of the digital world. They learn about the carbon footprint of AI, calculate their own digital impact, and design futuristic Eco-Cities that use technology to save the planet.

\vfill\null
\pamphletheading{Key Goals}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=SmaileLightGreen, colframe=SmaileGreen, boxrule=0.5pt, arc=2mm]
\small
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Learn:} About Data Centers.
    \item \textbf{Calc:} Digital Footprints.
    \item \textbf{Build:} Eco-Cities (Minecraft).
    \item \textbf{Act:} Reduce digital waste.
\end{itemize}
\end{tcolorbox}

% --- Panel 5: Activities ---
\pamphletheading{1. My Digital Footprint}
\textbf{Data Center Sim:}
Students role-play as servers processing data to understand the heat and energy involved. They calculate the impact of streaming vs. reading.

\pamphletheading{2. AI Eco-Builder}
\textbf{Design Challenge:}
Using Minecraft or paper, teams build a Green City.
\textbf{Features:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Smart energy grids.
    \item Efficient public transport.
    \item AI recycling systems.
\end{itemize}
