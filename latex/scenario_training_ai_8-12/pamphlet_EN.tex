\pamphletheading{Introduction}
Can computers see like us?

In this workshop, students (8-12) step into the role of AI scientists. They will collect real-world data (traffic signs), train an AI model to recognize them, and discover why diverse data is crucial for fair technology.

% --- Panel 5: Activities ---
\pamphletheading{1. Data Collection}
\textbf{Exploring Traffic Signs:}
Students photograph local signs and categorize them. They upload these to a shared folder to swap data with schools in other countries.

\pamphletheading{2. Training}
\textbf{Teachable Machine:}
Using the collected images to train a computer model.
\textbf{The Test:}
Does the AI recognize a stop sign from France if it was trained on signs from Italy?

% --- Panel 6: Reflection ---
\pamphletheading{3. Ethics}
\textbf{Debate:}
"Can AI replace human reasoning?"
Students discuss cases where AI made mistakes and reflect on the need for human supervision.
