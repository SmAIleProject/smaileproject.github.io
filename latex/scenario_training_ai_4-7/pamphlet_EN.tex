\pamphletheading{Introduction}
How do robots learn?

In this workshop, children (4-7) become "Robot Teachers." They will use picture cards to train a robot, learning that computers need examples (data) to understand the world—and sometimes they make funny mistakes!

% --- Panel 5: Activities ---
\pamphletheading{1. Train the Robot}
\textbf{The Data Game:}
Students sort cards (e.g., Apples vs. Carrots) into hoops. This explains how we "feed" data to computers so they can learn patterns.

\pamphletheading{2. Robot Guesses}
\textbf{Testing:}
The teacher (acting as a robot) picks a card and guesses.
\textit{"Is this a cat?"}
Students correct the robot: \textit{"No! That's a dog!"} This teaches that robots only know what they are shown.

% --- Panel 6: Creative ---
\pamphletheading{3. Human vs Robot}
\textbf{Sorting Jobs:}
Using "Emotion Cards," children decide: Can a robot feel happy? Or can it only do math?
\textbf{Result:} Robots help with tasks, but humans are best at feelings.
