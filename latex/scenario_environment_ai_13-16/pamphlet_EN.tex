\pamphletheading{Introduction}
The Hidden Cost of AI.

In this workshop, students (13-16) uncover the environmental impact of the digital world. They learn about the energy consumption of AI and data centers, calculate digital carbon footprints, and design innovative AI solutions to build a greener future.

\vfill\null
\pamphletheading{Key Goals}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=SmaileLightGreen, colframe=SmaileGreen, boxrule=0.5pt, arc=2mm]
\small
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Understand:} Data center energy.
    \item \textbf{Calculate:} Digital footprints.
    \item \textbf{Design:} AI for sustainability.
    \item \textbf{Reflect:} On personal habits.
\end{itemize}
\end{tcolorbox}

% --- Panel 5: Activities ---
\pamphletheading{1. Guess the Impact}
\textbf{Research Game:}
Students match digital activities to their environmental cost.
\textit{Streaming HD Video vs. Training an AI Model.}

\pamphletheading{2. AI Solutions}
\textbf{Design Mission:}
Teams invent AI-based systems to solve environmental problems.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Smart Energy Grids.
    \item AI for Waste Reduction.
    \item Efficient Transport Algorithms.
\end{itemize}

% --- Panel 6: Reflection ---
\pamphletheading{3. Reflection}
\textbf{Action Plan:}
